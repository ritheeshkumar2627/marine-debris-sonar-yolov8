The Mission

Beneath the Survey

Thousands of sonar frames come back from every survey, and slow, expert-driven inspection makes consistent debris detection almost impossible to scale. Real anomalies — unknown shapes, unfamiliar debris, objects nobody trained a model on — slip through manual review.

Varaha automates that screening. Computer vision detects, classifies, and validates anomalies across consecutive frames, filters out acoustic shadow and seabed clutter, then geolocates and ranks every confirmed target — so field teams open a prioritized list, not a folder of raw frames.
